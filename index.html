About us

Learning resources, made accessible.

A simple home for CBC notes, exams and revision materials.

Supporting learners at every stage

Elimu Hub is a learning portal concept designed to help Kenyan learners and families locate study materials in one organised place.

Resources are grouped by school level, grade and material type so learners can find the right content without getting lost.
